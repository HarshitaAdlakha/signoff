# Brand brief: Signoff, a Systems & Business "Engineers & the Future" case file

## Who is speaking
Paras Adlakha, an aerospace systems engineer who writes **Systems & Business** (newsletter, Medium, Reddit r/SystemsAndBusiness, Instagram, LinkedIn). The voice is a calm, credible engineer explaining a system to a smart colleague. Never a guru. Signoff was built with Harshita Adlakha for the Flip the Switch Hackathon (hosted by WeMakeDevs and DevTools Academy with Switch, 4-7 November 2026), Track 01: Best Business Workflow.

## Voice rules (follow exactly)
- Precise and calm. Say exactly what is true, then stop. Short lines, one idea per paragraph.
- Systems lens: requirements, interfaces, margins, feedback loops, trade-offs, failure modes, sign-off authority.
- Concrete engineering examples: a release needs a named signatory, a fix needs a preview before it is approved, a silent sensor is a failed sensor.
- Numbers with sources; say "about" when approximate.
- Learning in public: "here is what we built and what we found", not "here is what you must do".
- Credit sources: Sambasivan et al. (CHI 2021, data cascades), Parasuraman, Sheridan & Wickens (2000, levels of automation), Lee & See (2004, trust in automation), Amershi et al. (CHI 2019), Breck et al. (MLSys 2019), Schelter et al. (PVLDB 2018), O'Mahony & Shmoys (AAAI 2015), EU AI Act Article 14.
- NO hype words ("unlock", "10x", "game-changer", "revolutionary", "secret"), no corporate buzzwords (leverage, synergy), no clickbait.
- NEVER name any employer, aircraft program, customer or internship company. No defence topics.
- Do not describe any AI tool as a co-author or builder. The authors are Paras Adlakha and Harshita Adlakha.
- Thank the hosts by name: WeMakeDevs, DevTools Academy, and the Switch team at SandboxAQ.
- Long-form pieces end with "Views are my own."
- Maximum 5 hashtags anywhere.

## Signature lines for this project
- "Agents can watch the data. People still have to own the decision."
- "A fix without a preview is a guess. A fix without a signature is a liability."
- "A silent watcher is a failed watcher."
- "Gate the answer, not just the dashboard."

## Visual identity (Systems & Business design system)
- Engineering software meets an editorial magazine: every asset looks like a window on an engineer's screen (retro OS window frames, mono labels, halftone texture, a 48 px grid, node cards joined by thin connector lines).
- Base colours: Paper #F4F2EC background, Ink #111111 text and frames, Graphite #6E6B64 metadata, Grid #D9D5CB lines.
- This is an **Engineers & the Future** case file, so the single accent colour is **Terminal Pink #F48FB1**. Status colours only where a trust verdict is shown: trusted green, caveated amber, untrusted red.
- Type: Inter Tight (bold sans headlines), Instrument Serif italic for the voice, JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers.

## The one message
AI agents can do the watching, the preparing and the writing-up of data incidents. The decision to change or trust data belongs to a named person, in the room where the work happens, with the evidence in front of them. Signoff shows this on live Citi Bike data: agents detect bad data and propose a fix, only the data owner can sign it off (enforced by Switch, not by our code), and the dispatch analyst refuses to send a truck while the data cannot be trusted.

## Audience
Data and platform engineers, engineering managers deciding how far to let AI agents act, operations teams (mobility, logistics, fintech operations), and people building AI oversight and governance.
