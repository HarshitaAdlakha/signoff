An explainer for engineers: how Signoff puts AI agents and people in the same Switch rooms so bad data is caught, fixed only with a named person's sign-off, and never used to dispatch a truck while it cannot be trusted. Show the two rooms, the four agents and their roles, the 12-rule contract, the refused approval, the verified close and the three trust verdicts. Built by Paras Adlakha and Harshita Adlakha for the Flip the Switch Hackathon. End with the live app link parasadlakha.github.io/signoff/live.
